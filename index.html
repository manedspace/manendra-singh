Current Research

Scientometrics, AI, and the Future of Library Systems

My research brings together Library and Information Science with computational and AI-driven approaches to understand, organize, and evaluate scholarly knowledge.

I completed my Ph.D. at Banaras Hindu University, where I examined biotechnology research patterns across the BRICS nations. My subsequent work has expanded this scientometric perspective to areas including public health, artificial intelligence, research evaluation, and emerging scholarly fields.

Alongside research analysis, I work on the systems side of scholarly information — including multilingual information retrieval, digital libraries, intelligent library systems, NLP, topic modeling, and research-support applications.
